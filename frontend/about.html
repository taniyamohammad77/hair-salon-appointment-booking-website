About Amoré

A unisex salon, built around you

Amoré is a unisex hair studio where anyone can sit in the chair and get sharp, healthy hair — honest advice, calm energy and zero attitude.

Our concept

One studio, one standard: every guest gets the same care, whatever their hair type or style. We keep it simple — a clean space, a focused menu of services and stylists who listen before they pick up the scissors. Nothing gendered, nothing intimidating, nothing you didn't ask for.

What we offer

Precise haircuts, gentle colour work, blow-dries and treatments — a full menu covering different hair needs and styles. Whether it's a quick shape-up or a full colour day, the service list on our site shows exactly what each appointment includes, with real durations and prices.

Our approach

Every appointment starts with a conversation. We look at your hair, ask what you want, and tell you honestly what will work — before a single snip. You get our real opinion, not an upsell, and a finish that fits your everyday life.

The team

Three artists hold the Amoré standard: Maya Chen (Senior Stylist), Omar Haddad (Barber & Stylist) and Sofia Reyes (Colour Specialist). Each brings a specialty — cuts, beards, colour — and the same promise: listen first, cut with intention.
